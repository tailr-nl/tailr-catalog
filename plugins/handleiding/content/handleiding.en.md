# Getting started with AI and your business data

## The idea

Picture this: on Monday morning you ask your AI *"How did sales go last week, and which invoices are still open?"* and within seconds you have the answer. Straight from the source, without exports, without spreadsheets and without waiting for a colleague.

That works because at {{organisation}} you get access to the business data in your own AI tool, such as Claude, ChatGPT or Copilot. The accounting package, the ERP system, the CRM, the web shop, the website and the payment platform: everything you need for your work is reachable in one place.

And you can use everything your AI tool can do. Run analyses, draft documents, build dashboards, automate recurring work. You know best what makes your work easier, so you decide how to use it:

- **Quick answers**: *"Which customers ordered the most this quarter?"*
- **Connecting the dots**: *"Do the payments we received match what is in the accounts?"*
- **Your own routines**: *"Save this as my week start."* After that, one sentence gets you the weekly overview.
- **Your own tools**: have your AI build a dashboard or a handy helper, made for your work.

Behind the scenes, everything is checked. You only see what your role allows, sensitive data never reaches the AI, and every action is logged. So feel free to experiment.

## Getting started

1. Connect your AI tool under **Connect**.
2. Ask your AI: *"What can I access in our business data?"*
3. Ask a real question from your work.

## Good to know

- You can read everything you have rights to. Changing things is only possible if your role allows it, and your AI will ask for confirmation first.
- You confirm your own functions under **My functions**. They work in every AI tool and can never do more than you are allowed to.
- For your own tools and scripts, create a key under **My API keys**. Never share it.
- Under **My account** you can see where you are signed in. Don't recognise a device? Sign it out there and change your password.
- Something wrong, for example a lost laptop or a tool that behaves oddly? Under **My tools**, press **Revoke all AI access now**. All your AI tools and tools stop immediately; afterwards you simply connect them again.
- Does your AI say that AI is temporarily off (`ai_access_paused`)? Then {{admin}} switched off AI for everyone for a while. Tailr Core itself works as usual.
- Something not working? Your AI gives you a reference (`err_…`). Send it to {{admin}}.
